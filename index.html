Top Healthiest Options on the Chipotle Mexican Grill Menu

1. Salad Bowl with Lean ProteinStart with a bed of crisp romaine or supergreens, then add grilled chicken, steak, or sofritas for a protein boost. Pile on fajita veggies, black beans, salsa, and a scoop of guacamole for healthy fats and fiber.

2. Burrito Bowl with Brown RiceSkip the tortilla and go for a bowl with brown rice, black beans, chicken or sofritas, and lots of veggies. Brown rice adds whole grains and fiber, while fresh toppings keep calories in check.

3. Vegetarian SaladPerfect for plant-based eaters! Combine supergreens, black or pinto beans, fajita veggies, tomato salsa, corn, and guacamole for a nutrient-packed, filling meal.

4. Sofritas Tacos (Vegetarian)Choose soft corn tortillas, load them with sofritas, lettuce, pico de gallo, and a touch of cheese or guacamole for a light, protein-rich option.

5. Chicken Crispy TacoA crispy corn taco shell filled with grilled chicken, lettuce, and pico de gallo is a satisfying, lower-calorie choice.

6. Quesarito with VeggiesA unique twist for the adventurous: the Quesarito wraps your burrito in a cheesy quesadilla, filled with rice, beans, veggies, and your protein of choice. Opt for more veggies and lean protein to keep it balanced.

7. Guacamole Add-OnGuacamole is more than just a tasty topping-it's loaded with heart-healthy fats, fiber, and vitamins. Add a scoop to bowls or salads for extra nutrition.

8. Cilantro and SalsaFresh cilantro and salsa are low-calorie ways to add flavor, antioxidants, and vitamins to any meal.

Customizing for Special Diets

Keto-Friendly Choices

  • Start with a salad base or lettuce wraps

  • Add grilled chicken, steak, barbacoa, or carnitas

  • Top with cheese, sour cream, guacamole, and salsa

  • Skip rice, beans, and tortillas to keep carbs low

Whole30 & Gluten-Free

  • Choose salads or bowls with grilled meats and veggies

  • Avoid rice, beans, and cheese

  • Use salsa and guacamole for extra flavor

Low-Calorie Tips

  • Opt for bowls or salads over burritos

  • Choose lean proteins (chicken, sofritas)

  • Load up on veggies and salsa

  • Limit cheese, sour cream, and chips

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the healthiest proteins at Chipotle?Grilled chicken, steak, and sofritas are the leanest options, offering high protein with less fat.

How can I order low-calorie at Chipotle?Choose a salad or bowl, skip the tortilla, opt for brown rice or extra veggies, and use salsa instead of cheese or sour cream.

Is guacamole a healthy choice?Yes! Guacamole is packed with healthy fats, fiber, and vitamins. It's a great addition to salads and bowls.

Can I eat keto at Chipotle?Absolutely. Choose a salad base, add grilled meats, cheese, sour cream, and guacamole, and skip rice, beans, and tortillas.

Are there vegan options at Chipotle?Yes, sofritas (spicy tofu) is a vegan protein. Pair it with beans, rice, veggies, and guacamole for a filling meal.

Pro Tips for Ordering Healthy at Chipotle

  • Ask for extra veggies and salsa to boost nutrition and flavor.

  • Request half portions of rice or skip it altogether for fewer carbs.

  • Use guacamole and salsa as healthy, flavorful toppings instead of cheese or sour cream.

  • Check Chipotle's nutrition calculator to plan your meal in advance.

  • Share large portions or save leftovers for another meal.
